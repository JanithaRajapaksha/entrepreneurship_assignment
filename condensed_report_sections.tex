\subsection*{Section 4: Feasibility Study}

\textbf{1. Technical \& Engineering Feasibility:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=0.06em]
    \item \textbf{Vehicle Platform \& Payload Dynamics:} Converted 14.5-ft light commercial truck retrofitted with high-density acoustic insulation and waterproof vinyl flooring. Operating payload is strictly \textbf{$880\,\text{kg}$} (water, dual styling bays, power pack, 2 stylists, driver) on a 1.5-tonne chassis (GVW $\approx 3,500\,\text{kg}$), maintaining a $>50\%$ safety margin.
    \item \textbf{Electrical Power Architecture (AC Included):} Dual-tier power infrastructure: continuous load of \textbf{$2.9\,\text{kW}$} and peak load of \textbf{$7.5\,\text{kW}$}.
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=0.03em]
        \item \textit{Continuous Base Load ($1.3\,\text{kW}$):} 12,000 BTU rooftop inverter AC ($1.05\,\text{kW}$) maintaining 22$^\circ$C in tropical ambient heat, ventilation exhaust, LED lighting, and 12V diaphragm pumps ($250\,\text{W}$).
        \item \textit{Peak Thermal Demand ($6.0\,\text{kW}$):} Twin $2,000\,\text{W}$ ionic blow-dryers ($4.0\,\text{kW}$) and thermostatic water heating ($2.0\,\text{kW}$).
        \item \textit{Power Integration:} Primary $230\,\text{V}/16\,\text{A}$ shore connection at corporate parking bays, backed by a $5.0\,\text{kVA}$ pure sine hybrid inverter, $4.8\,\text{kWh}$ LiFePO4 battery pack, and 600W rooftop solar panels.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Hydraulic \& Water Systems:} Food-grade 350L clean water tank and 350L underslung greywater tank. Low-flow aerated nozzles consume \textbf{$8$--$10$ litres per wash} ($\implies 320\,\text{L}$ daily demand at 100\% capacity [32 clients/day]; zero midday refilling). Closed-loop couplings discharge greywater into municipal/building points with zero environmental runoff.
    \item \textbf{Operational Workflow \& Throughput Capacity:}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=0.03em]
        \item \textit{Schedule:} 16 operating days per month (Tuesday to Friday corporate peak).
        \item \textit{Operating Hours:} 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM (4.0 operational hours = 240 minutes daily).
        \item \textit{Express Cycle:} 15 minutes (9 min wash/massage/condition, 4.5 min express dry, 1.5 min sanitization).
        \item \textit{Workstations:} 2 active styling stations providing 16 slots/station/day = \textbf{32 clients/day maximum physical capacity} (512 services/month).
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\textbf{2. Preliminary Financial Feasibility:}
At the LKR 750 anchor fee, variable costs are controlled at LKR 150, delivering an \textbf{80.0\% contribution margin (LKR 600)}. Scaled monthly fixed costs are \textbf{LKR 193,900}, establishing an operational break-even of \textbf{323 services/month (20.2 clients/day, 63.1\% capacity utilization)}, confirming strong commercial viability with a 36.9\% safety margin.

\textbf{4. Investment Appraisal \& Milestone Gates:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=0.04em]
    \item \textbf{Commercial Returns:} Fleet expansion achieves full capital payback within \textbf{3.9 years}. Operating EBITDA scales from LKR 265,200 (Year 1) to LKR 1,460,640 (Year 3), elevating Return on Total Capital to \textbf{26.56\%} and Return on Founders' Equity to \textbf{97.38\%}.
    \item \textbf{Staged De-Risking Gates:} Capital deployment is unlocked through three disciplined milestones: Gate 1 (pre-sell 125 care packs before leasing vehicle), Gate 2 (formal corporate site parking permit before coachbuilding), and Gate 3 (achieve 19--20 clients/day pilot throughput before second-unit fleet expansion).
\end{itemize}
