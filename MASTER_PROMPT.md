## 2. Our Startup Idea — Mobile Hair-Washing and Drying Service

### Problem Identification

In Sri Lanka, many working women, particularly corporate employees, face difficulties maintaining regular hair-care routines due to demanding working schedules and household responsibilities.

Common difficulties include:

- Leaving home early due to long commutes.
- Returning home late after working hours.
- Limited time to wash, dry and style hair before work.
- Additional domestic and childcare responsibilities.
- The significant time required to dry and style long or thick hair.
- The inconvenience of travelling to conventional salons during a busy working day.

Traditional hair salons generally require customers to travel to a fixed location and allocate time for appointments.

This creates an opportunity for a more convenient service-delivery model.

### Proposed Innovation

Our proposed startup is a **mobile, professional hair-washing and drying service**, operated from a specially modified commercial vehicle equipped with essential salon facilities.

The vehicle would travel to predetermined high-demand locations, allowing customers to receive professional hair-washing and drying services close to their workplaces or other daily destinations.

The initial concept focused on corporate employees using the service during lunch breaks between 12.00 PM and 2.00 PM.

However, our initial market research suggests opportunities for morning and evening appointments as well. Therefore, the business should adopt a flexible, demand-driven operating schedule.

The startup's core value proposition is:

**To provide convenient, affordable, hygienic and time-efficient professional hair care by bringing salon services closer to customers instead of requiring customers to travel to a conventional salon.**

The innovation is primarily a combination of:

- Service-delivery innovation.
- Business-model innovation.
- Location-based convenience.
- Efficient mobile facility design.
- Digital appointment scheduling.
- Engineering integration of water, electrical, ventilation and wastewater-management systems.

Do not incorrectly present conventional salon equipment or an existing mobile salon concept as a completely new invention. Clearly identify the actual innovation and its commercial differentiation.

### Initial Target Market and Locations

The primary target customers are working women, especially office-based corporate employees with limited personal-care time.

The four locations initially considered are:

1. World Trade Center (WTC), Colombo.
2. Havelock City / Mireka Tower, Colombo.
3. Orion City, Colombo.
4. Access Towers, Colombo.

Our initial preference is to evaluate **WTC and Havelock City as primary launch candidates**.

WTC offers access to a concentrated corporate workforce, while Havelock City potentially provides access to office employees, residential customers, retail visitors and fitness-related customers.

Investigate and compare these locations using verified information, including customer access, potential demand, available facilities, competitors, operating restrictions, parking permissions and infrastructure requirements.

Do not assume that management approval, parking access or utility arrangements have already been obtained.

### Future Business Expansion

We recognize that hybrid and remote working arrangements could reduce long-term dependence on conventional corporate office locations.

Therefore, investigate opportunities to extend the service into:

- Gyms and fitness centres.
- Residential apartment communities.
- Shopping and lifestyle complexes.
- Business parks and coworking locations.
- Universities and professional institutions.
- Special events and pre-booked group services.

Develop a phased expansion strategy rather than proposing immediate expansion into every market.

### Proposed Customer Retention Strategies

Explore the feasibility of:

- Monthly membership packages.
- Digital loyalty or punch cards.
- Referral rewards.
- Corporate employee discounts.
- Prepaid appointment packages.
- BYOP (Bring Your Own Products).
- WhatsApp Business booking.
- Partnerships with property managers, employers, gyms and residential communities.

Assess the financial and operational implications of these strategies instead of assuming they will automatically improve profitability.
